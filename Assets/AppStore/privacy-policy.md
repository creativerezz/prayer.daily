# pray.daily privacy policy

Effective October 22, 2026.

pray.daily is a prayer-time app for iPhone and Mac made by Reza Jafar. It has no account, no advertising, and no analytics.

## What stays on your device

Your preferences, your chosen location, and the latest seven-day prayer schedule are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## Location

You choose your location in one of two ways:

- **Search a city or address.** Your search text is sent to Apple's geocoding service to find its coordinates.
- **Use my location.** The app asks iOS for your current location only when you tap this, and only after you grant permission.

## Prayer times

To calculate prayer times, the app sends your chosen latitude and longitude, calculation method and Asr convention to the AlAdhan API (aladhan.com) over HTTPS. Nothing that identifies you is sent with it. AlAdhan's handling of these requests is governed by its terms: https://aladhan.com/credits-and-terms

## Notifications

Prayer, Azan and holy-day reminders are scheduled locally by your device's operating system. No notification content leaves your device.

## What we don't do

We don't collect, sell or share personal data, track you across apps or websites, or use third-party SDKs.

## Your choices

You can turn reminders off in the app's Settings. You can revoke location or notification access at any time in your device's Settings.
